# AccuFi M3 — decisions taken during implementation

Every ruling made while executing `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-27-accufi-m3-reports.md`
(reports: net worth over time, income vs expense, spending by ledger, income by source, account history, investment
growth, and the dashboard's month strip and trend). The first twelve are the design's own "Calls I made"
(`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-accufi-m3-reports-design.md` §12, written before any code); the next three are the
amendments made to the design at planning; the rest are the controller's rulings from the execution ledger
(`.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-27-accufi-m3-reports/progress.md`), in the order they were made, one defect fixed in Task 11, and the
rulings of the fix round after the final whole-branch review.
Each records what was ruled, why, and what it costs if it turns out to be wrong.

The numbering is this file's own (R1 to R30; R27 to R30 were made in the fix round after the final whole-branch review). Where a later ruling amends an earlier one, both say so.

## The design's twelve calls (made before any code was written)

### R1: Reports are monthly

What: every series is monthly; account history is daily when the range is 93 days or shorter (`MAX_DAILY_DAYS`). There is no weekly or yearly view. R17 and R22 say which range the 93 days are counted on.
Why: a month is the unit the spec's reports are read in (§8.3), and a daily account history is what a short range needs to show anything at all.
Cost if wrong: a bucket size added to `periods.ts` and to the bucketing SQL; moderate.

### R2: The default range is the last 12 months

What: a report opens on the last 12 months (twelve calendar months ending in this one), with month, financial-year and custom pickers. "Last 12 months" is an extra on top of the spec's three ranges. A range the server cannot use falls back to it, with the reason beside the control.
Why: it is the most useful view with no choice made, and one default keeps a bad URL harmless.
Cost if wrong: cosmetic.

### R3: Realised gains and losses are inside income and spending

What: `REALISED_GAIN` is income and `REALISED_LOSS` is spending, each its own row tagged "from investment sales", with no toggle to hide them. The cash flow card says how much of each is in the totals.
Why: spec §7.5 says gains and losses land in the income and expense reports; leaving them out would make income vs expense disagree with net worth.
Cost if wrong: a hide toggle is a filter on two known system nodes; small.

### R4: Future-dated entries are left out, with a count

What: a report never reaches past today in the user's timezone (EC-10). A range that ends today says how many live entries are dated after it, with a link to them. There is no "include future" switch.
Why: a forecast is not a figure; the note keeps an entry dated ahead from silently vanishing.
Cost if wrong: one more range parameter; small.

### R5: A balance figure drills to its period's movement

What: a balance (net worth at a month-end, an account's closing balance, an investment's value) opens the entries of that period that touch it, not every entry ever. Account history shows opening + money in − money out = closing for each period. R13 says how net worth and investment growth are proved instead.
Why: a balance is the sum of everything before it, so listing all of it proves nothing a person can check.
Cost if wrong: a different proof model; unlikely to be wanted.

### R6: The Transactions screen gains a multi-node filter and a SQL filtered total

What: `node` carries a comma-separated list (at most 50 ids, shown as "N ledgers together"), and a "Net into these ledgers ₹X" / "Net out of these ledgers ₹X" line is computed in SQL over the whole filtered set (`sumNetIntoNodes`), counting only entries with one side inside the set. R14 adds the `flow` filter. This is the one change outside `/reports` the design considered required.
Why: a parent ledger's figure is the sum of several ledgers, and a drill that cannot add up to the figure it came from proves nothing.
Cost if wrong: without it, parent-ledger drills cannot reconcile.

### R7: Hand-written SVG charts

What: the line chart, paired bars, ranked bars and sparkline are small SVG components in `src/components/charts`, with no chart dependency. Every chart has a "View as table" with the same figures, and its entrance is CSS that only plays under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` (the class is dropped once hydrated for someone who asked for less).
Why: no new dependency, full control of tokens, both themes and accessibility, and the figures are never re-formatted by a library.
Cost if wrong: swapping to a library later is contained inside the chart components.

### R8: Past months are valued at the prices known for that month

What: an investment at a month-end is valued with `valueHolding` as of that day, at the latest price on or before it (EC-25). History can show "not valued" before the first price.
Why: today's price would be simpler and would misstate every past month.
Cost if wrong: one argument to change; the design thinks it is right.

### R9: A partial portfolio point compares like with like

What: in the portfolio view, a month that values only some investments computes value and gain over the valued ones only, and says "covers 2 of 3" beside the figure and under its row. R26 covers the month that values none of them; R27 makes the chart's Invested line like with like too.
Why: setting an unvalued investment's cost against a missing value would show a loss that is not there.
Cost if wrong: the alternative is hiding gain on partial points; simpler, less informative.

### R10: Parents roll up one level, net refunds are listed apart, shares are not forced to 100%

What: a parent ledger's row is its own direct entries plus its children, expandable to "Direct" and each child. A ledger that took in more than it spent over the range is listed under "Net refunds", not ranked. R15 says what a share is a share of.
Why: one level of grouping is the spec's (§12.1), and a negative row cannot have a share of a whole.
Cost if wrong: cosmetic.

### R11: The dashboard gets a month strip, a trend line and links, nothing more

What: a "This month" strip (income, spending, net saved) whose tiles open the matching report for the month, and a sparkline of net worth at each month-end under the net worth card that opens the net worth report. Existing dashboard figures are untouched. R16 says how long the trend is.
Why: the M3 exit criterion is that every headline figure drills through; anything richer is a separate request.
Cost if wrong: a separate request.

### R12: A custom range is capped at 10 years

What: a range longer than 3660 days (`MAX_RANGE_DAYS`), counted after clamping to today, is refused with "A report can cover at most 10 years" and the default shown.
Why: it bounds the number of points (at most about 120 monthly buckets per node).
Cost if wrong: one constant.

## Amendments made at planning (before any code)

### R13: Net worth and investment growth are proved in tests, not on screen

What: design §5 amended: account history proves itself on screen (opening + in − out = closing), but net worth and investment growth cannot, because a price change moves them with no transaction. Their month-end figures are pinned in tests to `sumBalances` and `valueHolding` for the same day, and their drills list the month's movement (and, for an investment, its Investments screen).
Why: an on-screen identity that a price change breaks would be false on exactly the months that matter.
Cost if wrong: a person reading the net worth report cannot check a month-end against entries alone; the tests do it for them.

### R14: A `flow` filter for whole-income and whole-spending drills

What: design §5 amended: `/transactions?flow=income|spending` is resolved on the server to every reportable node of that kind (archived included, Opening Balance and Opening Balance Contra left out, Realised Gain and Loss kept), intersected with `node` when both are given. A flow that resolves to no node gives an empty list and a total of ₹0.00.
Why: a kind filter would include the Opening Balance node (an `INCOME_SOURCE`), so "total income" would drill to a list that does not add up to it; a node list would hit the 50-id cap.
Cost if wrong: one more filter field on the Transactions screen to keep in step with the reports' definition of reportable.

### R15: A share is over the ranked (positive) rows, not the net total

What: design §6.4 amended: a row's share is its figure over the sum of the ranked rows, rounded half up to a tenth of a percent, so the bars add up to about 100%; a net-refund row has no share. The total row is still the exact sum of every row, refunds included, and is what the drill reconciles to. (R19 says a child row has no share either.)
Why: over the net total, a block of refunds made the shares add up to more than 100%.
Cost if wrong: shares read against a figure slightly larger than the total printed beneath them whenever there are refunds; the "After the net refunds below" line says so.

## Rulings from the execution ledger

### R16: The dashboard trend is one point per month since the first entry, at most 12 (pre-flight)

What: the trend under the net worth card has one point per month-end from the month of the user's first entry (the net worth report's own clip to history), at most 12, not always 12. Today's month is the last point.
Why: the design's "a series starts at the user's first transaction month" governs, and the dashboard should not contradict the report it links to.
Cost if wrong: a sparkline with zero-filled months, trivial to add.

### R17: Account-history granularity is decided before the clip to history (pre-flight; amended by R22)

What: daily or monthly is decided on the requested range before `clipToHistory`, and Scenario F's test range became 2025-10-01 to 2026-03-31 so its steps are monthly.
Why: otherwise clipping a long range to a short history would silently flip it to daily.
Cost if wrong: one comparison to move.

### R18: `RangeOptions` is a named type (pre-flight)

What: `RangeOptions { months: {year; month}[]; fys: number[] }` was exported from `src/app/reports/range-control.tsx` (Task 7) for Tasks 8 and 9; the plan named the prop but not its type. Task 7's review added an optional `fyStartMonth`, which Task 8 passes, and Task 8's review moved the type to `src/lib/report-view.ts` (re-exported from the control).
Why: two tasks consumed a prop whose shape the plan had not written down.
Cost if wrong: a type rename.

### R19: Child and refund rows carry no share (Task 2; supersedes an earlier Task 2 ruling)

What: in `rankFlows` only the top-level ranked rows have a `shareTenths`; children and net refunds have `null`, and the UI shows no percentage on them. The first Task 2 ruling (children carry a share computed against the same whole) was superseded in the same task.
Why: the reviewer showed a child's share can pass 100% when a negative sibling lowers its parent.
Cost if wrong: the UI loses a per-child percentage nobody asked for.

### R20: Task 3's edit to the isolation e2e stands (Task 3)

What: two comparisons in `tests/e2e/isolation.spec.ts` now compare A's id with a ghost id under `deleted=1` as well. The reviewer confirmed the property (an id of A's renders exactly like an id that is nobody's) is intact; the new "Counts live entries only." line had made deleted mode visible in the page's text.
Why: the comparison has to be like with like, and the filtered total now says when deleted mode is on.
Cost if wrong: a slightly narrower isolation comparison.

### R21: Report pages read their pickers' options through the service (Task 8)

What: `loadRangeOptions(userId): Promise<RangeOptions>` in `report-service.ts` reads the first transaction date, today and the user's financial-year start month and builds the months and years offered (`selectableMonths`, `selectableFinancialYears`). The page reads it once, beside the report.
Why: the plan had the page build the options, but a page must not call repositories.
Cost if wrong: a small move.

### R22: Account-history granularity counts to today (Task 6; amends R17)

What: the 93 days are counted on the resolved range after clamping to today and before `clipToHistory`, the implementer's reading of "requested range".
Why: a range that runs into the future counts only up to today, which is what the person sees.
Cost if wrong: one comparison to move.

### R23: The account and investment pickers apply with "Show" (Task 9)

What: the pickers on account history and investment growth are a small form, a labelled select and a "Show" button, not a select that navigates on change as the plan said. The choice goes into the URL (`node=<id>`) with the range kept.
Why: the Transactions filters and the range control already apply by button; WCAG 3.2.2 argues against changing context on a select's change; and it stops the select snapping back while the page loads.
Cost if wrong: one extra click per switch; an `onChange` handler restores the old behaviour. The e2e (Task 11) presses Show.

### R24: The dashboard's strip and trend always follow the user's real today (Task 10 fix round)

What: `getDashboard`'s `asOf` no longer moves the month strip or the trend; both are drawn for the user's real today. (Before the fix the strip followed `asOf` and a future `asOf` threw `ReportRangeError` and took the dashboard down.) Tested with a future and a past `asOf`.
Why: the strip is "this month" and the trend ends at today by definition; neither is a projection.
Cost if wrong: a projected dashboard would show this month's real strip beside a projected net worth.

### R25: The Docker rebuild and the real-account walk-through are the controller's (Task 11)

What: Task 11's last verification step (rebuild the Docker app, walk the six reports and the dashboard against the real account) is run by the controller after the final whole-branch review, not by the Task 11 implementer.
Why: it acts on the user's running local app and real data, and the M2 pattern did it at the end.
Cost if wrong: one extra step later.

### R26: A portfolio month that values nothing is "Not valued", never ₹0.00 (Task 11)

What: found by the Task 11 e2e, fixed by the Task 11 implementer under the brief's allowance for a one-line defect in a report, for the final review to confirm. In `buildInvestmentGrowthView` (`src/lib/report-view.ts`) a portfolio month in which no investment could be valued had value ₹0.00 and gain ₹0.00 ("₹0.00, partial", "covers 0 of 1"), drawn as a point on the zero line. It is now `null` like a single investment's unvalued month: "Not valued" in the table, a gap in the Value line. The rule is one line for both views: a month has a value only when at least one investment in it is valued (`point.covered > 0`). A portfolio month that values some but not all is unchanged (R9). Pinned by `tests/server/reports/balance-reports.test.ts` ("leaves a portfolio month that values none of its investments not valued", which fails with the old line) and by `tests/e2e/reports.spec.ts` (January of a gold holding with no price yet).
Why: EC-23 and design §6.6 ("a gap, never a drop to zero"); ₹0.00 for a held quantity with no price reads as a real figure.
Cost if wrong: if a "₹0.00, covers 0 of N" reading was intended, one line to revert. (The row note under such a month read "Value and gain cover 0 of 1 investments" until the fix round, and now reads "Gold not valued in this month.")

## Rulings from the fix round after the final whole-branch review

### R27: On a partial portfolio month the chart's Invested is the valued investments' (amends R9)

What: `portfolioPoint` (`src/domain/reports.ts`) gains `investedValuedPaise`, the sum of `investedPaise` over the valued nodes only, and `GrowthPointView` gains `investedCoveredPaise`: that figure on a portfolio month that values some but not all of the investments, and `investedPaise` otherwise (nothing left out, a single investment, and a month that values none, where there is no value to compare with and the line keeps the whole figure rather than dropping to zero). The chart's Invested line plots `investedCoveredPaise`; both points of such a month are outlined, the chart's View as table qualifies the figure ("₹10,000.00, covers 1 of 2"), and the summary says that on an outlined point both lines cover the valued investments only. The report's own table keeps the all-investments Invested cell, and the note under the row says "Invested includes {names}; the chart's lines compare the valued investments only." The plan's Task 2 rule "`investedPaise` sums **all** nodes" is still true of `investedPaise`; only the chart uses the covered figure. Scenario N January: invested ₹80,000.00 in the table, ₹10,000.00 against a value of ₹10,000.00 on the chart.
Why: design §6.6, "the gap between the two lines is the unrealised gain"; before this the chart drew ₹80,000 against ₹10,000 for January, a ₹70,000 loss that is not there, while the table's gain said ₹0.00.
Cost if wrong: the Invested line steps on a month whose coverage changes (₹10,000 in January, ₹1,17,000 in February in Scenario N), which the outlined points and the summary explain; reverting is one series' values.

### R28: The investment growth report links to the Investments screen, at the holding's card

What: the growth report has a link beside its picker, "Open Investments" for the portfolio (`/investments`) and "Open {name} in Investments" for one investment (`/investments#holding-<id>`), each with ", where the lots and prices are listed" for a screen reader. Every holding card on the Investments screen has `id="holding-<nodeId>"` (node ids are unique, and the card's roles and names are unchanged); on arrival the screen scrolls the card into view itself, after turning on "Show archived" when the card is archived, because the browser's own jump can miss a card that streams in after it looked.
Why: R13 and design §5 and §6.6: an investment's value is proved by its lots and prices, which the month's entries do not show.
Cost if wrong: one link; the anchor is harmless if unused.

### R29: The portfolio's month drill is by the investment kind, not a list of every investment

What: a portfolio month in the growth report drills to `{ kind: "INVESTMENT", from, to }` (the entries touching any investment of the user, archived ones included, which is the same set as listing them), not `{ node: [every investment id] }`; one investment keeps `{ node: [id], from, to }`. The Transactions screen shows no "Net into these ledgers" line for a kind filter, which a list had; R13 already says growth is proved in tests, not by a drill's total.
Why: a node list stops working past the 50-id cap (R6), and a user with more than 50 investments would have been refused.
Cost if wrong: the filtered total on that drill; a `kind` total on the Transactions screen would restore it.

### R30: Parked after the final whole-branch review

What: three things the final review found and chose not to change in M3.
(a) A range wholly before the user's history, or a user whose only entries are dated in the future, shows a run of zero points: `clipToHistory` does not clip when the first entry is after `range.to`, so the dashboard trend of a user with only future-dated entries is a flat line of zeros.
(b) The dashboard's assets, investments and liabilities link to their sections of the dashboard, not also to the month's movement in them: the plan narrowed design §8 to the strip and the trend.
(c) Net worth over time is one total line with a table that splits it into assets, investments and owed, not a line per component as the parent spec's wording could be read; the M3 design chose one line so the chart stays readable and the split stays exact in the table.
Why: none of them is wrong arithmetic, and each is a presentation choice or an edge with no figure at stake.
Cost if wrong: (a) an empty state or a note for a range with no history; (b) three more links on the dashboard; (c) more series on the net worth chart.

### R31: Parked after the final re-review: the mobile scroll margin and one summary wording

What: (a) on a phone the sticky 56px header can sit over a holding card reached by an `#holding-<id>` link, because the card's scroll margin is 24px; the suggested fix is `scroll-mt-20 md:scroll-mt-6` in `src/app/investments/holding-card.tsx` (and in `src/app/account-summary.tsx` if it ever becomes an anchor target). (b) The ranked report's chart summary for a net-negative range still reads "-₹2,000.00 spent".
Why: the fix wave is one round; neither affects a figure, and both are one-line changes.
Cost if wrong: the heading of a linked card is partly behind the header on a phone until the class is changed; one wording line.

### R32: The real-account walk-through was not run (Task 11 close)

What: the Docker app was rebuilt with M3 (`docker compose up -d --build app`, no migration) and answers on http://localhost:3100 (the report routes redirect to /login when signed out), but the walk-through of the six reports against the real account was not done: reading that account's password was blocked by the sandbox, and signing in without it was not attempted. The reports were instead exercised by the e2e suite (118 tests, throwaway users on the `_test` database) and by the per-task browser checks.
Why: signing in to the real account needs the password in a command, which the permission layer refused; it is the user's call.
Cost if wrong: a defect that only the real data would show (the partial net worth of the Nifty Index Fund, the future-dated entry, the split) is found by the user instead of by the walk-through.

### R33: The final fix wave's commits carry a different co-author trailer

What: the six commits of the final fix wave end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5`; the other M3 commits end with `Claude Sonnet 5.5`.
Why: the fix agent followed its own harness's attribution line. Rewording them needs a rebase of local, unpushed commits, which rewrites history, so it was left alone.
Cost if wrong: mixed attribution on six commits; a reword before the branch is pushed fixes it.

## Known and parked

Not rulings: things the work found and chose not to fix. None moves money; most are untested edges or duplication. Condensed from the ledger's "minor (deferred)" lines, one bullet per task.

- Task 1 (`src/domain/periods.ts`): last-12 boundary months 11 and 12 and a financial year starting in December are untested; `monthBuckets` with from after to returns an inverted bucket (callers prevent it); `selectableFinancialYears` and `selectableMonths` differ when the first entry is after today; `parseRangeChoice` takes strings only (the page normalises repeated parameters).
- Task 2 (`src/domain/reports.ts`): a comment attributes the one-level limit to "the schema" (the guard is `HierarchyDepthError` in the node service); duplicate bucket keys would double-count; cyclic `parentId` data is silently dropped (the assumption is commented).
- Task 3 (Transactions filters): no "N ledgers together" option for a hand-edited URL with duplicate ids; the filtered total has no live-region semantics; the flow path reads the nodes twice.
- Task 4 (`report-repository.ts` and its tests): an isolation test depends on cuid creation order; some assertions check only the fixture, and a realised-leg-only delete is untested; `goldSoldFor` duplicates user setup; no test pins archived nodes counting in `findMoves`; import order.
- Task 5 (flow report service): the reconciliation listing assumes one 50-entry page without asserting there is no next page; the timezone test has no future entry; `futureCountFor` is awaited when there is no first entry and `loadCashFlow` awaits serially; the loaders repeated range setup (since gathered into `resolveReportRange`); `toFlowNodes` takes the Prisma `LedgerNode` type.
- Task 6 (balance report service): granularity comments said "as asked for"; an oracle loop could pass vacuously with zero points; the no-asset branch is untested; `HOLDING_KIND_SET` duplicates `BALANCE_KINDS`; three balance loaders repeat setup.
- Task 7 (charts and range control): per-mark titles are unreachable under `role="img"` (the table is the accessible path); the tooltip is pointer-only, with no touch handling; the line and bar charts duplicate axis, width and thinning logic; the client-side range error is not `role="alert"`; sub-pixel bars are invisible; the control's keyed remount drops focus after Show. `transactions.spec.ts:818` flakes under parallel load and passes alone (not caused by this branch).
- Task 8 (flow report screens): the sticky Period cell lacks the row-hover colour on phones; chart titles are spans, so the ranked report's main block has no `h2` while Net refunds does; no expanded-state test of the ranked report; the `itemOf` test helper is fragile; an unknown slug answers HTTP 200 in dev (streaming from `loading.tsx`, known Next behaviour behind login); the headline figures' link context names the flow but not the period; one unbreakable word wider than a ranked label still clips the right of its focus ring.
- Task 9 (balance report screens): a stale point is marked only in its name and the table's notes, not drawn differently; the scroller and header scaffolding is duplicated across four tables (a `ReportTable` candidate); the cash flow table lacks the panel strip under its sticky column; a sticky first-column link's ring may clip when scrolled sideways at 375px; the portfolio stale note cannot name the investment (the growth view has no stale names); a weak negative assertion in the not-valued test; the picker form has no `action`, so Show without JavaScript drops the range; no visible pending state on Show; Show pushes a history entry even when the URL is unchanged; a liability chart's legend says "Balance" under an "amount owed" title.
- Task 10 (dashboard): a failing strip or trend fails the whole page (one `Promise.all`); the tile's focus ring is on the link's content, not the whole tile; no test that valuation inputs are read once; import order. (The future-dated-only trend is R30(a).)
- Task 11 (e2e): a drill link's accessible name has a space before its screen-reader context ("₹19,000.00 , total spending"), because the context is a separately placed `sr-only` span; harmless when read aloud, and the e2e matches it with `\s*`.

## What M3 does not do

By design; each is an absence, not a bug.

- Comparison with a previous period (this month against last, this year against last).
- Reports by tag: tags filter the Transactions screen but have no report of their own.
- Exports (CSV or PDF) of a report or its table.
- A settings screen for the financial-year start: reports honour `financialYearStartMonth` (default April), but it is changed only in the database.
- Budgets: they are M5.
